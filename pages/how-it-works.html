How KampoStay Works

Search, pay securely, and move in — with a full refund if the room is nothing like the listing.

1. Search

Filter by university, rent, distance, amenities, and more across Kenya.

2. Verify & pay

Choose verified listings and pay deposits via M-Pesa, card, or bank transfer.

3. Move in

Book a viewing or reserve the room. If it does not match the listing, you get 100% refund.

100% refund if reality does not match the listing

Paid on KampoStay and the place is completely different from the photos, amenities, or location promised? Report within 48 hours of your first visit and we refund you in full.
